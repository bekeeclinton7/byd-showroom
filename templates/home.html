{# ---------------- FAQ ---------------- #}

Questions

Pre-order FAQ

Everything you need to know before reserving.

Is the {{ deposit_from|cad }} deposit really refundable? {{ icon('plus') }}

Yes. You can cancel at any point before signing your final purchase agreement and receive the full deposit back to your original payment method.

How does the pre-order discount work? {{ icon('plus') }}

Each model carries a launch discount off the estimated MSRP, from $1,000 on the Seagull to $3,500 on the Han. It is locked in when you reserve before the launch deadline.

Are BYD vehicles eligible for federal EV rebates? {{ icon('plus') }}

Under current rules, China-built vehicles are not eligible for the federal EV incentive. Some provincial programs may apply; your retailer will confirm eligibility at delivery.

Are prices final? {{ icon('plus') }}

Prices shown are estimated MSRPs in CAD and exclude freight, PDI, licensing and fees. Taxes shown at checkout are estimates based on your province.

Where will I pick up my vehicle? {{ icon('plus') }}

Initial retail locations are planned for Toronto, Vancouver, Montréal and Calgary. Home delivery is available in every province and territory.
